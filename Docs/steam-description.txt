[b]A research window of its own, built on UI Rethemed - Core, with research sorted into tech-level tabs and each tab laid out as a tidy, readable tree.[/b]

In some cases the result is not strictly linear, but I believe it makes the tree much more readable in highly modded games.

[h2]Research window[/h2]
[list]
[*]Drag to pan, use the mouse wheel to zoom, and press Fit to see the whole tab. Each tab remembers its view.
[*]Cards show their era's color and their state at a glance (finished, available, being researched, locked), with up to three unlock icons, techprint and study counters, a progress strip and a hover card listing what the project needs and what it unlocks without further research.
[*]Cards come in Compact, Normal and Large sizes.
[*]Lines between projects are smooth curves, and selecting a project lights up its whole prerequisite chain.
[*]Era bands tint each era's columns, with the era's title pinned along the top, on tabs laid out era by era.
[*]A research queue in a foldable panel on the left: shift-click a project to queue it along with any prerequisites it still needs, and drag to reorder. When the current project finishes, the next one starts. Folded, the queue still shows as icons.
[*]A foldable details panel on the right shows what the selected project needs (met or not), what it unlocks now and with more research, what it leads to, and buttons to research or queue it.
[*]A bar along the top shows the current project's progress and roughly how long it has left, at the colony's recent research pace.
[*]Search finds projects by name or by anything they unlock, and chips show or hide locked, finished and future-era projects.
[/list]

[h2]Tabs and layout[/h2]
[list]
[*]Technologies are routed to their tech level's tab: Primitive, Neolithic, Medieval, Industrial, High Industrial, Late Industrial, Spacer, Ultra and Archotech. Empty tabs are hidden.
[*]Industrial is split three ways: Industrial, High Industrial (needs the Hi-Tech Research Bench) and Late Industrial (needs the Multi-Analyzer).
[*]Entity knowledge technologies are moved to the Anomaly tab.
[*]No overlapping cards and fewer crossing lines. Follow-ups sit to the right of what they need, and branches fill the space beside their prerequisites instead of trailing off the end of the tab.
[*]A technology is raised to the era of its prerequisites, so a follow-up never sits in an earlier era than what it needs. Its research cost changes to match.
[*]Tab tooltips show completed projects and research points.
[*]Schematics can teach technologies from every era tab.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Research window[/b]: full screen (on by default), open on the current project, tab project counts, curved lines, whole-chain highlight, era bands, time left, and hiding Node Research's emergence lines.
[*][b]Cards[/b]: card size, unlock icons, techprint and study counters, progress strip and hover card.
[*][b]Tabs[/b]: Combine All Tabs puts everything except Anomaly, Gravship and Save Our Ship 2 on the Main tab, with each era in its own block from left to right, with or without a gap and lines between eras. Combine Industrial merges High and Late Industrial back into one Industrial tab.
[*][b]Colors[/b]: color cards by era, or use plain cards. Each era's color is picked on UI Rethemed - Core's settings page.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Needs [b][LFS] UI Rethemed - Core[/b].
Works with [b]Node Research[/b] (foundation technologies wear a gold crown and rule, its vanilla menu button opens this window, and a button in this window's title bar goes back), [b]Semi Random Research - Continued[/b] and [b]Research Queue[/b] (their tree buttons open this window, with a button back in its title bar; they choose what to research, so this mod's queue is off while either is active), [b]Save Our Ship 2[/b] (its Ship and Archotech tabs keep their projects), and [b]ProgressionLESS[/b] and [b]VFE Tribals[/b] (their research lands in the right tabs).
Not compatible with Research: Organized, or with other research tab organizers, because they control the same system.
Safe to add to an existing save. Removing it from a save logs one harmless error about its saved queue.
